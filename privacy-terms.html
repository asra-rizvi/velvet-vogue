Terms & privacy

The short version of how we handle your orders and your data.

Last updated: October 2026

Privacy: what we collect

Velvet Vogue runs entirely in your browser. There is no server behind this site, so nothing you enter is sent anywhere.

  • Account details: your name, email and a hashed password, saved on this device only.
  • Optional details: phone number, delivery address and profile photo, if you add them.
  • Orders, cart and wishlist: saved on this device only.
  • Contact messages: checked in your browser and kept on this device.

Cookies and storage

We do not use cookies. We use your browser's local storage to remember your cart, wishlist, account and orders. Clearing your browser data removes all of it. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so that service will see a normal page request.

Your choices

You can update your details any time in My Account. To remove everything, clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Terms of sale

  • Prices are shown in Sri Lankan rupees (LKR). The price you see in your cart at checkout is the price you pay.
  • An order is confirmed when you see your order number. We may cancel an order if an item turns out to be unavailable, and will tell you if so.
  • Delivery and returns are covered in Shipping & Returns.
  • Promo codes cannot be combined and may expire.

Questions

Email info@velvetvogue.lk or use the contact form.
