Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about ordering, delivery and returns. Can't find yours? Message us.

Ordering

Do I need an account to shop?

You can browse and fill your cart as a guest. You need an account to check out, so we can show your order history. Your guest cart moves into your account when you sign in.

How do I pay?

Cash on delivery is available now: pay the courier when your order arrives. Card payments are planned.

How do promo codes work?

Enter a code in your cart before checking out. The discount applies to the items, not to delivery.

How do I choose my size?

Pick a size and colour on the product page. If you are unsure, send us your measurements through the contact form and we will help.

Delivery

How long does delivery take?

Colombo orders arrive in 2–3 business days. The rest of Sri Lanka takes 4–7 business days.

How much is delivery?

LKR 350 per order. Delivery is free when your items total LKR 5,000 or more, before any promo discount.

Do you ship internationally?

Not yet. We deliver within Sri Lanka only.

How do I check my order status?

Sign in and open My Account. Each order shows its current status: pending, processing, shipped or delivered.

Returns and refunds

What is your return policy?

You can return an item within 14 days of delivery if it is unworn, unwashed and has its tags attached. See Shipping & Returns for the full steps.

When will I get my refund?

Once we receive and check your return, we refund you within 7 business days.

Your account

I forgot my password. What do I do?

Automatic password reset is not available yet. Send us a message from the contact page and we will help you get back in.

How do I change my details or password?

Open My Account, then choose Account Details or Password & Security.
