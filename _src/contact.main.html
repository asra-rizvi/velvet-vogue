Frequently Asked Questions

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery within Colombo takes 2–3 business days. Island-wide delivery takes 4–7 business days.

What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 14 days of delivery. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and in original packaging with tags attached.

How do I track my order?

Once your order is dispatched, you'll receive an email with a tracking number. You can also check your order status in your account dashboard.

Do you ship internationally?

Currently we ship within Sri Lanka only. International shipping is coming soon — stay tuned for updates!
